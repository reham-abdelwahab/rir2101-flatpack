interlocked · 8 slices · 8 parts · 1 sheet(s)

How the labels read
  X-2 = the 2nd slice of the X family, Y-4 = the 4th of the Y family, each counted from the low end (a slice auto-fix added is numbered after the family's own)
  the two families cross at right angles and half-lap into each other: X slots drop in from one side, Y from the other
  Every label starts with CW11, short for chair_waffle_1-10: parts of two models cut together are never mixed up.
  A part too big for the sheet is cut into pieces and the piece number comes last: Z-3-2 is the second piece of Z-3.
  Each label is engraved on its own part where it fits, else beside it on the sheet with a line pointing at it.
  A small part may carry its name without the short name in front: that was all it had room for.
